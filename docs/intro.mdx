## How I work

- I start with who the reader is and what they're trying to do.
- I work closely with engineers and product teams to get the details right, then test the instructions as a user would.
- I write in plain, consistent language and organize content so it's easy to scan.
- I use Markdown, Git, and static site generators such as Docusaurus and MkDocs to keep documentation close to the product.
