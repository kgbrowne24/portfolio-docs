**Context:** A help article for *Brightpath Scheduler*, a fictional appointment app. Support tickets show users often fail to set up recurring appointments. <br />
**Goal:** Identify what makes the article hard to use, then rewrite it.

## Original text

> Recurring appointments can be configured by the user in the appointment creation workflow. It should be noted that the recurrence functionality is accessed via the Repeat field, which may need to be enabled depending on account settings. Once the recurrence pattern has been selected, the appointment series will be generated. Users who experience issues should contact support.

## Audit findings

| Issue | Example | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passive, wordy language | "can be configured by the user in the appointment creation workflow" | Use direct instructions: "Select **New appointment**." |
| No steps | The task is described but not broken into actions | Use a numbered procedure |
| Vague prerequisite | "may need to be enabled depending on account settings" | Say who can enable it and where |
| Missing result | "the appointment series will be generated" | Describe what the user will see |
| Dead-end ending | "contact support" | Add specific troubleshooting for common problems |

## Rewrite

**Set up a recurring appointment**

Use recurring appointments for meetings that happen on a regular schedule, such as a weekly check-in.

**Before you begin:** The **Repeat** field must be turned on for your account. If you don't see it, ask your account admin to turn on **Recurring appointments** in **Settings > Scheduling**.

1. Select **New appointment**.
2. Enter the title, date, and time.
3. Select **Repeat**, then choose how often the appointment happens: **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**.
4. Choose when the series ends: on a specific date or after a number of appointments.
5. Select **Save**.

Your calendar now shows each appointment in the series.

**Troubleshooting**

- **I don't see the Repeat field.** Ask your account admin to turn it on, as described above.
- **Only one appointment appears.** Check that you chose an end date or number of appointments that's later than your first appointment.
- **I need to change just one appointment.** Open that appointment and select **This appointment only** when you save.

## Result

The rewrite replaces five wordy sentences with a short intro, a prerequisite, a five-step procedure, and three troubleshooting entries. It tells users what they'll see at the end and how to fix the most common problems without contacting support.
